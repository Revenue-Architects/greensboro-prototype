# Greensboro Town Website: Concept Prototype

A clickable concept prototype prepared by Revenue Architects for the Town of Greensboro, VT website committee.

**This is not the official town website.** All names, dates, and documents are placeholders.

## Review
Open the live link, click through the pages, and use "Send feedback" at the top of any page.

Suggested path: Home, Meetings & Records, a meeting page, Boards & Committees, Selectboard, Sign up for alerts, and "Staff view" (how Brett and Josh would post).

## Files
- `index.html`: the entire prototype (single self-contained file)
- `robots.txt`: keeps the prototype out of search engines
